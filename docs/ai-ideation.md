# AI Ideation

## Purpose

AI was used as a divergence partner during the ideation stage to explore possible solutions for traffic congestion and inefficient fixed signal timing at Oppanakara Street, Coimbatore.

The goal was not to accept every AI-generated idea directly, but to compare alternatives, identify practical solutions, and refine the final concept based on the real-world problem.

## Initial Problem

The existing problem identified at Oppanakara Street includes:

- Peak-hour traffic congestion
- Long vehicle queues
- Long waiting and idling times
- Uneven traffic demand across lanes and directions
- Inefficient fixed signal timing

## AI Ideation Prompt

The following type of prompt was used to generate possible solutions:

"Act as a design thinking and smart mobility expert. Generate practical AI-based solutions for reducing traffic congestion, vehicle waiting time, and inefficient signal timing at a busy urban intersection such as Oppanakara Street in Coimbatore. Consider real-time traffic detection, vehicle classification, lane-wise traffic demand, safety, pedestrians, and practical implementation."

## AI-Generated Solution Ideas

### Idea 1: AI-Based Adaptive Traffic Signals

Use computer vision and traffic analysis to detect vehicles, estimate traffic density, and dynamically allocate green time based on current traffic demand.

**Decision:** Adopted.

**Reason:** This directly addresses the identified problem of fixed signal timings and changing traffic demand.

### Idea 2: Real-Time Traffic Prediction

Use historical and current traffic data to predict short-term traffic surges.

**Decision:** Partially adopted as future scope.

**Reason:** Prediction can improve the system, but the current prototype focuses first on observing and responding to current traffic conditions.

### Idea 3: Smart Parking Guidance

Use AI to identify available parking spaces and guide vehicles toward them.

**Decision:** Rejected.

**Reason:** Parking availability is not the primary problem identified for this project. The project focuses specifically on traffic signal efficiency and congestion at Oppanakara Street.

### Idea 4: Public Transport Priority

Give buses priority during selected traffic conditions.

**Decision:** Reserved for future development.

**Reason:** It could improve public transport movement, but the current prototype focuses on general traffic demand and adaptive green-time allocation.

### Idea 5: Pedestrian-Aware Signal Management

Include pedestrian crossing requirements when determining signal timing.

**Decision:** Considered and retained as a safety requirement.

**Reason:** Adaptive signal control should not optimize vehicle flow at the expense of pedestrian safety.

## Final Selected Direction

The selected solution is an AI-based adaptive traffic signal management system.

The proposed system follows this process:

Traffic Observation
→ Vehicle Detection
→ Vehicle Classification
→ Traffic Analysis
→ Adaptive Signal Decision
→ Optimized Traffic Flow

## Why This Solution Was Selected

The solution was selected because it directly connects the identified traffic problem with an actionable intervention.

Instead of using only fixed signal timings, the proposed system responds to current traffic conditions and dynamically reallocates green time according to traffic demand.

## AI's Role

AI was used to:

- Generate multiple possible solution directions.
- Explore alternative approaches.
- Stress-test the proposed concept.
- Identify additional factors such as pedestrian safety and prediction.
- Help refine the final solution.

Human judgment was used to select ideas based on relevance to the actual problem, feasibility, safety, and project scope.

## Current Status

The adaptive traffic signal concept has been developed into a digital prototype using the SmartFlow AI traffic dashboard.

Further user validation will be used to identify improvements to the prototype.
