# Prototype

## Prototype Name

SmartFlow AI - Adaptive Traffic Signal Management Prototype

## Prototype Type

Digital low-to-mid fidelity prototype

## Project Location

Oppanakara Street, Coimbatore

## Purpose

The prototype demonstrates how an AI-based traffic signal management system could observe traffic conditions, analyze traffic demand, and support adaptive signal timing.

## Prototype Link

https://smartflow-ai-traffic-euzx.bolt.host

## Core Workflow

The proposed workflow is:

Traffic Observation
→ Vehicle Detection
→ Vehicle Classification
→ Traffic Analysis
→ Adaptive Signal Decision
→ Optimized Traffic Flow

## Main Concepts Demonstrated

### Traffic Observation

The system is designed to work with traffic conditions observed at the intersection.

### Vehicle Detection

Vehicles can be detected from traffic observations to understand the number and position of vehicles.

### Vehicle Classification

The proposed system considers different vehicle categories such as:

- Two-wheelers
- Cars
- Buses
- Commercial vehicles

### Traffic Analysis

The system considers traffic density, queue length, lane-wise demand, and directional traffic flow.

### Adaptive Signal Management

When traffic demand is high, the system can allocate more green time to the congested approach.

When traffic demand is low, unnecessary green time can be reduced to avoid inefficient signal phases.

## Safety Considerations

Traffic optimization should operate within safety constraints.

Pedestrian crossing requirements and safe signal operation must be maintained while optimizing traffic flow.

## Prototype Objective

The prototype is intended to communicate and demonstrate the concept before further development and real-world testing.

## Current Limitations

The current prototype is a demonstration of the proposed concept. Real-world deployment would require:

- Reliable traffic sensing
- Validated AI models
- Integration with traffic signal controllers
- Extensive testing
- Safety validation
- Appropriate permissions and infrastructure

## Next Step

The prototype will be shown to at least three real users to collect feedback.

Their feedback will be used to identify usability issues, missing features, concerns, and possible improvements.
