# Problem Statement

## Project Location

Oppanakara Street, Coimbatore

## The Problem

Oppanakara Street experiences traffic congestion during peak hours. Frequent vehicle queues reduce traffic throughput, while long waiting times and vehicle idling cause frustration for road users.

Traffic demand can also vary rapidly across lanes and directions. Fixed traffic signal timings may not respond effectively to these changing conditions, resulting in inefficient allocation of green time.

## Key Problems Identified

### 1. Peak-Hour Congestion

Heavy traffic during peak periods can create long vehicle queues and reduce the efficiency of traffic movement.

### 2. Long Waiting Times and Idling

Fixed signal timings can cause vehicles to wait unnecessarily even when traffic demand differs between approaches.

### 3. Uneven Traffic Flow

Traffic demand can change rapidly across lanes and directions, while static signal schedules may not adapt to these changes.

## Defined Problem

How might we use AI-based traffic observation and analysis to dynamically allocate traffic signal green time according to changing traffic demand at Oppanakara Street, while maintaining safety for all road users?

## Proposed Direction

The project proposes an AI-based adaptive traffic signal management system that observes traffic conditions, detects and classifies vehicles, estimates lane-wise demand and traffic density, and dynamically adjusts signal timing based on current traffic conditions.

## Goal

The goal is to create a responsive traffic signal management approach that can reduce unnecessary waiting, improve traffic flow, and make better use of available green time.
